# Language.fi Legal and IP Positioning

## Core Principle

Language.fi does NOT claim ownership of language, words, or sentences. The protocol provides a registry for protocol-native assets, not legal ownership of ordinary language.

## What Users Own

### Registry Entry Ownership

When a user registers a word or sentence in the Language.fi registry, they own:
- **Protocol-native asset representation**: A unique hash-based identifier within the protocol
- **Transferable registry entry**: The right to transfer the registry entry to another wallet
- **Formula value calculation**: The calculated value based on primitive prices at time of minting
- **On-chain record**: A permanent record of creation and ownership (if on-chain)
- **Composability rights**: Ability to combine with other protocol assets

### What Users Do NOT Own

Users do NOT own:
- **The word or phrase itself**: Language remains in the public domain
- **Trademark rights**: No exclusive right to use the word in commerce
- **Copyright ownership**: No copyright over the linguistic content
- **Exclusive use**: Others can still use the word in ordinary language
- **Legal name control**: No control over personal names or identities
- **Dictionary rights**: No claim to the word's definition or meaning

## Registry Entry vs Word Ownership

### Registry Entry (Protocol-Native)

- **Scope**: Within Language.fi protocol only
- **Nature**: Transferable asset within protocol
- **Rights**: Protocol-native representation, transfer rights
- **Enforcement**: Protocol-level rules only
- **Duration**: As long as protocol exists

### Word Ownership (Legal)

- **Scope**: Legal system, general use
- **Nature**: Trademark, copyright, or other IP rights
- **Rights**: Exclusive commercial use, legal enforcement
- **Enforcement**: Legal system
- **Duration**: Varies by IP type (trademark renewal, copyright term)

**Critical Distinction**: Registry entry ownership is completely separate from legal word ownership. Owning a registry entry for "APPLE" does NOT give you Apple Inc.'s trademark rights.

## Trademark Positioning

### No Trademark Claims

Language.fi explicitly does NOT claim:
- Trademark rights to any word or phrase registered
- Exclusive commercial use of registered terms
- Rights to prevent others from using language
- Rights to enforce against ordinary language use

### Trademark Conflicts

Users are responsible for:
- Ensuring their registered terms do not infringe existing trademarks
- Not using registered terms to impersonate brands
- Not claiming trademark rights through registry ownership
- Understanding that registry entry ≠ trademark

### Protocol Stance

The protocol:
- Does NOT verify trademark status before registration
- Does NOT prevent registration of trademarked terms
- Does NOT enforce trademark law
- Reserves right to remove infringing content upon notice

## Copyright Positioning

### No Copyright Claims

Language.fi does NOT claim:
- Copyright over words, phrases, or sentences
- Copyright over linguistic content
- Copyright over ordinary language
- Copyright over user-generated content

### Public Domain

Language.fi operates on the principle that:
- Words and phrases are generally in the public domain
- Short phrases are not copyrightable
- Language is a shared human resource
- Registry does NOT create copyright

## Personal Names

### Name Registration

Users can register personal names, but:
- No claim to identity ownership
- No right to impersonate individuals
- No legal name control
- Protocol-native representation only

### Privacy

The protocol:
- Does NOT verify identity before registration
- Does NOT require real-name verification
- Does NOT store personal data beyond wallet addresses
- Respects privacy by design

## Content Moderation

### Prohibited Content

Language.fi reserves the right to refuse registration of:
- Slurs and hate speech
- Illegal content
- Harassment
- Threats of violence
- Child exploitation
- Other content that violates terms of service

### Moderation Process

- Protocol may quarantine or remove prohibited content
- Users may appeal removal decisions
- Moderation decisions are final
- No refund for removed entries
