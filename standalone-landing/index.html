THE METHOD

Every price keeps its evidence close.

The model compares observed character frequency with a published English baseline, then applies visible floor, demand, and rarity terms. The point is not to hide uncertainty behind a number. It is to make the number inspectable.

01 / SAMPLE

Observe public text

Connected sources are sampled and normalized into a current corpus.

02 / APPRAISE

Compare demand

Observed frequency is measured against the declared baseline and formula.

03 / VERIFY

Expose the ledger

Source state, model inputs, refresh time, and limitations remain visible.

TRANSPARENCY FIRST

A laboratory, not an exchange.

Language.fi explores programmable language using experimental reference values and Solana devnet primitives. Devnet assets have no monetary value. No displayed value is investment advice or a promise of liquidity.
