How KPIs Are Calculated

  1. Real-time data from Dexscreener (30+ pairs) and Gate.io (2,230+ tickers)
  2. Count letter occurrences in cryptocurrency trading pair symbols
  3. Calculate frequency per total characters analyzed
  4. Measure source diversity (unique exchanges containing the character)
  5. Track topic diversity (unique trading topics)
  6. Advanced metrics: Compression Alpha, Entropy Resistance, Oracle Flux Density
  7. Liquidity Shadow and Semantic Pressure Ratio calculations
  8. All data is REAL - no simulation, no mock data, live cryptocurrency market data
